Our Story

A Legacy of Sweetness from the Heart of Kewra Bazar

For over five decades, Tikri Junction has been the beating heart of sweetness in Ballia district. What started as a humble shop with a single recipe has grown into the most trusted name in traditional Indian confectionery.

Our signature Tikri sweet has become synonymous with Ballia itself. Made with pure desi ghee, fresh milk, and a secret family recipe passed down through generations, every piece of Tikri carries the authentic taste that has made us famous across the entire district.

From weddings to festivals, celebrations to everyday cravings — Tikri Junction has been the first choice for every sweet occasion. We believe that great sweets are made not just with ingredients, but with love, tradition, and an unwavering commitment to quality.
